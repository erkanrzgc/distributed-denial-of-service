## Educational Purpose

This toolkit is developed for **educational and defensive security research purposes only**. It is designed to help security professionals, system administrators, and researchers:

- Understand DDoS attack vectors and methodologies
- Test the resilience of their own infrastructure
- Develop and validate defensive strategies
- Learn about network security in controlled environments
